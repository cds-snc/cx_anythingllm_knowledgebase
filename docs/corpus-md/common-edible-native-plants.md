# Common Edible Native Plants of Vancouver Island

**Source:** Capital Regional District Native Plant Resources — Vancouver Island

Lists edible native plants found on Vancouver Island, including berries, greens, and roots.

---

Common Edible Native Plants sonpuitnoumo

CODE: (BF) berry/fruit (SS) stalk/stem/leaves (N) nut (Bb) bulb
Perennials
- Nodding onion (Allium cernuum) - (Bb) Eaten raw or cooked
- Hooker's onion (Allium acuminatum) - (Bb) Bulb roasted, cooked or dried
- Camas (Camassia quamash, C. leichtlini)- (Bb) Bulb roasted, cooked or dried
- Tiger lily (Lilium canadensis) - (Bb) Bulb cooked or dried, flowers are also edible
- Chocolate lily (Fritellaria lanceolata) - (Bb) Bulb cooked or dried
- Yerba beuna (Satureja douglasii)) — (SS) Tea
- Stinging nettle (Urtica dioica) - (SS) Soup, tea, stew, wine beer
- Woodland and Coastal strawberry (Frageria vesca and F. chiloensis) — (BF)
- Miner's lettuce (Montia perfoliata) - (SS) Edible leaves for salads
